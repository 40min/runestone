# Passiv form 😴 — when the subject is just lying there, being acted upon

Passive voice = something is *done to* the subject. The subject didn't do it. The subject had it done *to them*. Like a pizza — it didn't bake itself. 🍕

Swedish gives you **two weapons**:

1. **-s passiv** — slap an -s on the verb. Done. 🪄
2. **bli + perfekt particip** — use *bli* + the participle. Two words but more dramatic. 🎭

Both mean the same thing. Use whichever one doesn't make you spiral.

---

## Weapon 1: -s passiv 🪄

**Formula:** verb stem + **-s**, conjugated in whatever tense you need.

The -s stays glued to the verb no matter what. It's the clingy passive.

| Tense | Swedish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Presens | Maten **serveras** klockan fem. | The food is served at five. |
| Preteritum | En man **rånades** igår kväll. | A man was robbed last night. |
| Perfekt | Biblioteket **har renoverats** under året. | The library has been renovated this year. |
| Futurum | Vinsten **kommer att delas** lika. | The prize will be split equally. |

**The rule:** just conjugate the verb normally, then add -s. That's it. Resist the urge to overcomplicate it. 🛑

⚠️ One word. No agreement. No gender drama. The subject can be en-word, ett-word, or a herd of elk — the -s does not care. It's blissfully unaware of Swedish noun genders.

---

## Weapon 2: bli + perfekt particip 🎭

**Formula:** bli (conjugated) + the perfect participle of the verb.

*Bli* carries the tense. The participle carries the meaning. They're a crime duo.

| Tense | Swedish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Presens | Maten **blir serverad** klockan fem. | The food is (being) served at five. |
| Preteritum | En man **blev rånad** igår kväll. | A man was robbed last night. |
| Perfekt | Biblioteket **har blivit renoverat** under året. | The library has been renovated this year. |
| Futurum | Vinsten **kommer att bli delad** lika. | The prize will be split equally. |

Use *bli* passiv when you want to stress **the moment something changes** — a completed event, a new state, a dramatic shift. Think of it as the passive with feelings. 💅

---

## The Agreement Problem™ 😬

Here's where *bli* passiv gets spicy. The participle is an adjective in disguise, so it **must agree** with the subject's gender and number.

| Subject type | Ending | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **en**-word (singular) | -ad / -d / -en / -t | en man **rånad** |
| **ett**-word (singular) | -at / -t / -et | ett bibliotek **renoverat** |
| **plural** | -ade / -da / -na | böckerna **renoverade** |

That's why it's *renoverat* (ett bibliotek) but *rånad* (en man). The participle saw the gender coming from a mile away and adjusted accordingly.

With -s passiv? Zero agreement. The -s just vibes. 😎

---

## Side-by-side comparison 🥊

| | -s passiv | bli + particip |
|---|---|---|
| **Word count** | 1 (or 2 with *har*) | always 2+ |
| **Agreement** | ❌ never | ✅ always |
| **Gender drama** | none | some |
| **Vibe** | neutral, everyday, chill | change, result, emphasis |
| **Sounds like** | bureaucratic memo | breaking news |

> **Rule of thumb:** when in doubt, use -s passiv. It's always acceptable and it never picks fights over grammar. Use *bli* passiv when the change or result is the whole point. 🎯

## More examples to steal 🕵️

| Swedish | English | Weapon used |
|---|---|---|
| Glaset krossades. | The glass was broken. | -s |
| Glaset **blev krossat**. | The glass got broken. (emphasizes the crash 💥) | bli |
| Brevet skickades igår. | The letter was sent yesterday. | -s |
| Brevet **har blivit skickat**. | The letter has been sent. | bli |
| Inget beslut har fattats ännu. | No decision has been made yet. | -s |
| Skyltarna **blir tvättade** på fredag. | The signs are being washed on Friday. | bli |
| Dörren låstes upp. | The door was unlocked. | -s |
| Dörren **blev upplåst** av vakten. | The door was unlocked by the guard. | bli |

## The "by whom" trap 🪤

Both passives can name the doer using **av**:

- Boken skrevs **av Strindberg**. — The book was written by Strindberg. ✍️
- Boken **blev skriven av Strindberg**. — The book was written by Strindberg. ✍️

Same meaning. The -s version sounds slightly more literary; the *bli* version sounds like you're reporting it on the news.

---

## The ultra-cheat 🧠

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Who receives the action? That's your subject. |
| 2 | Is the doer unimportant or unknown? → Use passive. |
| 3 | Choose weapon: -s for neutral, *bli* for emphasis. |
| 4 | If *bli*: conjugate it for tense, then add the participle. |
| 5 | If *bli*: check subject's gender/number, agree the participle. |
| 6 | If -s: add -s. You're done. Go home. 🏠 |

Remember: **-s = one piece, no fuss. bli + particip = two pieces, with feelings.**
