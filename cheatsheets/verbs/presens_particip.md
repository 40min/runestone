# 🏃 The Swedish Present Participle: A Word That Never Stops Running 🏃

## (Presens Particip — the verb that moonlights as EVERYTHING)

---

## 🔨 How to BUILD one (It's embarrassingly easy)

### Rule 1 — Verb ends in **-a**? → Rip off the -a, slap on **-ande**

| Infinitive | Chop it | + ande | Meaning |
|------------|---------|--------|---------|
| sjunga | sjung- | **sjungande** | singing |
| sova | sov- | **sovande** | sleeping |
| leka | lek- | **lekande** | playing |
| springa | spring- | **springande** | running |

> 💡 **Tip**: -a verbs are the majority. They see -ande and they say "yes, that's me."

### Rule 2 — Verb ends in **any other vowel**? → Keep it, add **-ende**

| Infinitive | + ende | Meaning |
|------------|--------|---------|
| gå | **gående** | walking |
| förstå | **förstående** | understanding |
| sy | **syende** | sewing |
| le | **leende** | smiling |
| bo | **boende** | living/residing |

> 😤 **Why -ende?** Because you can't have two -a endings crashing into each other. That would be **sjungaande** and Swedish people would physically wince.

## 🎭 Four Jobs, One Word (The Overachiever)

The present participle is the ultimate side-hustler. Same form, four completely different careers.

---

### Job 1: ADJECTIVE 🎨
*Describing a noun — but it NEVER changes. Not for gender. Not for number. Not for definiteness. Nothing.*

| Example | Translation |
|---------|-------------|
| en **springande** man | a running man |
| ett **springande** barn | a running child |
| två **springande** män | two running men |

> 🤘 **This is the lazy adjective.** While other Swedish adjectives are busy changing forms every five seconds (*stor → stort → stora → store...*), the present participle sits there in one form going "I'm good, thanks."

---

### Job 2: ADVERB ⚡
*Describes HOW something happens. Also never changes (it's on the same lazy plan as the adjective).*

| Example | Translation |
|---------|-------------|
| Hon kom **gående** | She came walking |
| Hon kom **springande** | She came running |

> 😂 **Fun fact**: "Hon kom springande" literally means "She came running" — perfect for describing your Swedish friend who's always five minutes late but arrives very energetically.

---

### Job 3: NOUN 📦
*Now it HAS to do some work. Nouns inflect.*

**ett leende** (a smile):

| Form | Swedish | Trick |
|------|---------|-------|
| Indefinite singular | **ett leende** | just the word |
| Definite singular | **leendet** | + t (it's ett-word) |
| Indefinite plural | **flera leenden** | + n |
| Definite plural | **leendena** | + na |

**ett meddelande** (a message — yes, your phone notifications):

| Form | Swedish |
|------|---------|
| ett meddelande | a message |
| meddelandet | the message |
| meddelanden | messages |
| meddelandena | the messages |

> 📱 **Real talk**: "meddelanden" is why your Swedish phone says *du har 3 meddelanden* and not just *messages*. You're welcome.

---

### 🤯 SPECIAL CASE: **boende** — The Identity Crisis Word

*boende* as a **thing** (a dwelling) → inflects like a normal noun:

| ett boende | boendet | flera boenden | boendena |
|------------|---------|---------------|----------|

*boende* as a **person** (someone who lives somewhere) → **only the article changes, the word STAYS THE SAME**:

| en boende | den boende | flera boende | de boende |
|-----------|------------|--------------|-----------|

> 🙃 "The word refused to change for people but happily changes for buildings."
> Swedish logic: **buildings get full inflection; humans just get a new article.**

---

### Job 4: PREPOSITION 🔗
*Exactly one (1) famous example: **angående***

| Word | Origin | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| **angående** | angå (to concern) | regarding / concerning |

> 📧 *Jag ringer angående tjänsten som snickare.*
> *I'm calling regarding the carpenter position.*

> 🏢 **angående** is basically the Swedish "RE:" in an email subject line. Very formal. Very efficient. Very Swedish.

## 🚨 The Same Word, Three Lives: **leende**

| Sentence | Role | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| Hon har ett **leende** ansikte | Adjective | before noun, no change |
| Hon gick **leende** förbi | Adverb | how she walked |
| Han gav henne ett **leende** | Noun | it's the smile itself (ett-word) |

> 🤯 *Same spelling. Three jobs. Swedish is completely fine with this. You should be too.*
