# 🎁 var, varsin, varsitt, var och en — Swedish for "each person gets their own"

## (Because apparently Sweden refuses to share things without full grammatical precision)

---

## The Big Picture 🗺️

Swedish has three ways to say "each person gets something." English just says "each" and calls it a day. Swedish looked at that and said: *"Not specific enough. Where exactly does the number go? Does the noun agree? Are we talking about the individual or the pile? Let's make three separate forms for this."*

| What you want to say | Tool |
|---|---|
| Everyone gets **[number] things** | **var** (after the noun) |
| Everyone gets exactly **one [noun]** | **varsin / varsitt** (before the noun) |
| Every **individual person** | **var och en / var och ett** |

---

## Tool 1: var 🔢 — "each gets X"

**Position:** goes at the **end**, after `[number] + noun`

**Formula:** `[noun] + [number] + [noun?] + var`

**What it means:** the number applies to each person. Like saying "five pens — each" instead of "five pens total."

| Swedish | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Barnen fick **fem pennor var**. | Each child got five pens. | 5 per child, not 5 total |
| De lånade **två böcker var**. | Each person borrowed two books. | noun there |
| Vi fick **500 kronor var**. | Each person got 500 kronor. | number only, no noun |
| Ni kan ta **en glas var**. | You can each take one glass. | one per person |

> 🎯 **The key insight:** `var` doesn't go before anything. It parks itself after the number (and optional noun) like someone who always has to have the last word.

> 🤔 **Without `var`:** *"Barnen fick fem pennor"* = the children got five pens (total, shared, tragic).
> **With `var`:** *"Barnen fick fem pennor var"* = each child got five pens (personal property!).

---

## Tool 2: varsin / varsitt 🎯 — "one each" (agrees with noun gender)

**Position:** goes **before** the noun, replacing the article

**Formula:** `varsin/varsitt + [noun]`

**What it means:** exactly one per person. No more, no less. Very fair. Very Swedish.

| Noun type | Word | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **en**-word | **varsin** | varsin penna (en penna) |
| **ett**-word | **varsitt** | varsitt äpple (ett äpple) |

| Swedish | English | Noun gender |
|---|---|---|
| Barnen fick **varsin penna**. | Each child got a pen. | en penna → varsin |
| Barnen fick **varsitt äpple**. | Each child got an apple. | ett äpple → varsitt |
| Ni kan ta **varsin glas**. | You can each take a glass. | en glas → varsin |

> 🧐 **varsin vs var:** `varsin penna` = one pen per person. `tre pennor var` = three pens per person. Use **varsin/varsitt** when it's specifically *one*, use **var** when it could be any number.

> 🚨 **Don't mix them up:** `varsin` comes BEFORE the noun (like a normal adjective). `var` comes AFTER the number (like a rebel).

> 💡 **Memory trick:** **var**s**in** → "var" (each) + gendered suffix. **-sin** for en-words (sIn = sIngular en). **-sitt** for ett-words (sitt sounds like "sit", ett-words make you sit and think harder 🪑).

---

## Tool 3: var och en / var och ett 🎖️ — "each individual person"

**Position:** can stand alone or before `av + noun/pronoun`

**Formula:** `var och en/ett [av + noun]` — the `av + noun` part is optional

**What it means:** *varje enskild person* — every single individual, one by one. Not "all of them at once" — each. one. separately.

| Noun/context | Word |
|---|---|
| **en**-word (or general person) | **var och en** |
| **ett**-word | **var och ett** |

| Swedish | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Var och en** (av er) måste göra läxorna. | Each person must do the homework. | *av er* optional |
| Jag måste ringa **var och en** av mina vänner. | I must call each of my friends. | one by one, not a group call 😅 |
| Det är upp till **var och en** om man vill träna. | It's up to each person whether to train. | individual autonomy |
| Det är upp till **var och en** om man vill åka buss. | It's up to each person whether to take the bus. | same construction |
| **Vart ett** av barnen har en egen bok. | Each of the children has their own book. | ett-word version (note: *vart ett*, not *var och ett*) |

> ⚠️ **Watch out!** With ett-words, the form is **vart ett** (not *var och ett*). *Vart* is the ett-form of *var*. Because of course there's one more irregular thing to remember. 🙃

> 📞 **The "one by one" feel:** *"Jag måste ringa var och en av mina vänner"* means calling them individually. If you said *"alla mina vänner"* it sounds like a group call. Same people, very different energy.

---

## The Overlap Zone 🔄 — three ways to say the same thing

Swedish loves giving you options you didn't ask for. All three of these mean the same thing:

```
Barnen har varsin bok.          (Tool 2 — one each, agrees with noun)
Vart och ett av barnen har en bok.  (Tool 3 — each individual child)
Varje barn har en bok.          (boring, no drama)
```

→ There's one book per child. The books are not shared. Sweden has spoken. 📚

## Side-by-Side Decision Chart 🥊

| Question | Answer | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| Each person gets **multiple** things? | `[number] + [noun] + var` | **var** at the end |
| Each person gets **exactly one** thing? | `varsin/varsitt + noun` | **varsin/varsitt** before noun |
| Emphasizing **individuals** (one by one)? | `var och en/ett [av + noun]` | **var och en/ett** |

---

## Quick Grammar Summary 🧠

| Word | Position | Agrees with | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **var** | after `number + noun` | nothing | tre böcker **var** |
| **varsin** | before en-noun | en-words only | **varsin** penna |
| **varsitt** | before ett-noun | ett-words only | **varsitt** äpple |
| **var och en** | standalone / before *av* | en-words / people | **var och en** av er |
| **vart ett** | before *av* | ett-words | **vart ett** av barnen |

---

> 🎉 **The takeaway:** Swedish distributes items with surgical precision. `var` = tallies the count per person. `varsin/varsitt` = one each, gender-matched. `var och en` = full individual focus, possibly with a clipboard.
>
> English: *"Everyone gets one."* 🤷
> Swedish: *"Varsin? Varsitt? Var och en av? What is the noun's grammatical gender and how many items per person, please?"* 🇸🇪📋
